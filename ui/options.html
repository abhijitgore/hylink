HyLink

Hover a hyperlink to get a menu of ways to open it.

How it works

  1. Rest the pointer on a link in a page's text. Moving restarts the wait, so reading never triggers anything, and links in navigation bars, headers and sidebars are skipped.
  2. Three dots appear just past the link.
  3. Move onto them and they open into the action bar. Press Esc, scroll, or move away to dismiss it.

HyLink sends nothing anywhere — no analytics, no network requests. Your settings sync through your Chrome profile and never leave it.

Appearance

The grip keeps links readable while you browse — the full menu only appears once you move onto it (or hold the modifier key).

Timing

Moving the pointer restarts this, so sweeping across text while reading never triggers the menu.
